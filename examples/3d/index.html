Wireface SDK: 3D examples

Each one is a single short HTML file: view its source.

  • hello

    The smallest face: a wireframe that follows the pointer and smiles.

  • skins

    The built-in skins, swept onto the wireframe, and a slider from wireframe to skin.

  • expressions

    Every expression, two custom ones made with defineExpression, moods and states.

  • talk

    Recorded clips with their mouth tracks: lip sync you can hear.

  • live

    Lip sync from your microphone or any audio as it plays.

  • photo

    Make a skin from your own photo, in the browser, and save it.

  • config

    Config files: load, edit, validate, apply.

And the demo, with every setting on one page.
