A robot's own face

A rigged model from a file: two eyes, no mouth, nothing that moves them. Gemini Pro looked at a picture of its head and said where its eyes are, that it has no mouth (and where one should go) and no nose. ModelFace rigs that: lids that blink over its own eyes, a glint in each that follows you, and a mouth laid on its head that lip-syncs. Gemini's answer is saved with the model (RobotFace.features.json); with the source and a Gemini key on the dev server, Gemini looks again, live.

Animation

Its face

The model's animations play underneath, and the face's nods and turns are added on top of them, so it can wave, dance and talk at once. Model: RobotExpressive by Tomás Laulhé (CC0), from the three.js examples, given room for a face: a taller head, with smaller eyes and brows set inside it (tools/robot-face.mjs).

const robot = (await new GLTFLoader().loadAsync(url)).scene;
const features = await (await fetch(
  'models/RobotFace.features.json')).json();
const face = new ModelFace({ ...features,
  head: findBone(robot, /^head$/i) });  // lids, glints, a mouth
face.gazeAt(camera);
await face.say(clip, { audioContext });
