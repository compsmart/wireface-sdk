Looking at things

face.gazeAt(target) takes anything in the world: a point, a camera, another head. The eyes go first and converge on it; past a little way the head turns too, up to turn radians, and the eyes do the rest.

face.gazeAt(orb);                     // an Object3D
face.gazeAt(new THREE.Vector3(1, 2, 0)); // a point
face.gazeAt(camera, { turn: 0.4 });    // eyes more, head less
face.gazeAt(null);                     // ahead
