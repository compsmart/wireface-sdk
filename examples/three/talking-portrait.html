A talking portrait

A FaceScreen on Wireface 2D, which makes any picture of a face talk. The picture is the texture of a canvas in a gilt frame on the wall: it speaks its line, and its eyes follow you round the room. Pick another picture from 2D's thirteen art styles.

const screen = new FaceScreen({ engine: '2d',
  character: 'eleanor', resolution: [416, 520],
  config: { framing: 'cover' } });
painting.add(screen.bind(new THREE.Mesh(
  screenGeometry({ width: 0.46, height: 0.575 }),
  screen.material())));
screen.gazeAt(camera);
await screen.useCharacter('hazel', { transition: 0.9 });
