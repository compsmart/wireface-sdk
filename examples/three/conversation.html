Two characters talking

Turn-taking: whoever speaks looks at you, the listener looks at the speaker, nods along (listening is a face state of its own) and reacts at the end. Each line is a recorded clip with its expressions cued along it.

async function line(speaker, listener) {
  listener.gazeAt(speaker.head);
  speaker.gazeAt(camera);
  listener.face.view.event({ topic: 'percept.audio.partial' });
  await speaker.speak({ audioContext, onCaption });
  listener.face.express('amused', 2.5);
}
